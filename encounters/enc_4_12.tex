\begin{boxtext}
  A stone's throw away in the forest, something like a white shawl waves in the
  biting wind.  A rattling sound comes from beneath it.
\end{boxtext}

The white material is part of a large web, built by dozens of small \glspl{crawler}.

The little \glspl{crawler} hate the wind, so they don't attack unless assaulted
directly.

\begin{boxtext}
  Beneath the white mesh, the bones of deer, badgers, and \pgls{digger} lie on
  the ground.  A few still stuck in the webbing bash together to make the
  rattling sound.
\end{boxtext}

The webbed area stretches across half a mile, so if the \glspl{pc} travel
through this area, they cannot return the same way without a full-on attack.

\paragraph{After \pgls{interval},}
the troupe regenerate 4~\glspl{mp} from the winds.
