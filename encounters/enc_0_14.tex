\begin{boxtext}
  Today the branches drip, ferns drip, hats drip, and any donkeys or carts also drip.
  The world has little swirls of shining movement whenever the wind dislodges more drips from the persistent rain.
\end{boxtext}

Once of these shining swirles comes from \pgls{woodspy}.
