\begin{boxtext}
  The heavy rain beats the ground, pulling up old smells from the mud on the
  \gls{lonelyRoad}.  The scent begins `earthy', then turns strange like the
  outhouse of a family with a disease.
\end{boxtext}

Stirges overwhelmed a weak \gls{basilisk}, then began to eat its body.  The
rain has interrupted them, so the troupe will only see \pgls{basilisk}'s
corpse.  The hide remains, but the inside is hollow.

If the troupe interfere, the stirges emerge and attack, but suffer a -2~Penalty
to movement and any rolls while the rain continues.
